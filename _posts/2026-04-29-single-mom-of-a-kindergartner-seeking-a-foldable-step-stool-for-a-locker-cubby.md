## Shopping List for a Foldable Step Stool (Locker Cubby Use)

- **Foldable Step Stool** (primary item)
- **Non-slip rubber feet** (pre-installed or add-on)
- **Compact carry or storage bag** (optional but recommended)
- **Wipe-clean surface material** (e.g., plastic, coated wood, or antimicrobial)
- **Weight capacity at least 150 lbs** (for adult use when needed)
- **One-hand fold mechanism** (quick release)
- **Anti-pinch hinge guards** (child-safe design)

## Buying Guide

### **Why a Foldable Step Stool for a Locker Cubby?**
A kindergartner’s locker cubby is often tall, narrow, and hard to reach. A foldable step stool solves this by providing stable height access without taking up permanent floor space. For a single mom, portability and safety are critical—this stool must be easy to pack for school drop-offs, field trips, and after-school activities.

### **Key Considerations for Your Purchase**

#### **1. Folded Size and Portability**
Look for a stool that folds flat (ideally under 2 inches thick) so it slips easily into a backpack side pocket or a tote. Many foldable stools collapse to roughly 10 x 12 inches—small enough for a cubby shelf or a car seat pocket. **Bold tip:** Avoid models with bulky hinges that prevent flat storage.

#### **2. Stability and Non-Slip Grip**
A kindergartner’s balance is still developing. The stool must have **rubber feet** that grip tile or linoleum school floors. Some stools come with textured stepping surfaces (e.g., molded ridges or foam pads). Test by pressing down—if it wobbles, skip it. **Safety note:** A stool that slides even an inch can cause a fall.

#### **3. Easy, One-Handed Folding Mechanism**
You’ll likely be holding a lunchbox, backpack, or your child’s hand. Look for a stool that folds with **one pull or push**—no complex latches or multiple steps. Bonus points for auto-locking when open (prevents accidental collapse). For a single mom, time is precious; every second counts at school drop-off.

#### **4. Weight Capacity for Adult Use**
While your child weighs 40–50 lbs, you may need to use the stool to reach a high cubby or organize classroom supplies. Choose a stool rated for **at least 150 lbs**. Many budget stools cap at 100 lbs, which may not hold you safely. Check the product label—preferably 200+ lbs for durability.

#### **5. Material and Cleanability**
School cubbies collect dirt, glue, snack crumbs, and marker stains. Opt for:
- **Hard plastic** (e.g., polypropylene): Lightweight, wipes clean, never splinters.
- **Coated wood** (e.g., bamboo with non-toxic finish): Elegant but heavier; ensure it’s sealed against moisture.
- **Antimicrobial coatings**: Some models add silver-ion protection (helpful for shared school spaces).
- **Avoid** unfinished wood or fabric-covered stools—they absorb spills and odors.

#### **6. Child-Safe Design Details**
- **Rounded corners** (no sharp edges that catch clothing or skin).
- **Anti-pinch hinges** with covers (prevents little fingers from getting caught during folding).
- **No sharp seams or burrs**—run a hand over the stool’s surface before buying.
- **Low profile when open**: Step height around 6–8 inches is ideal for a kindergartner’s leg length. Too tall (10+ inches) can tip them forward.

#### **7. Additional Practical Features**
- **Carrying handle** built into the stool (even better than a separate bag).
- **Color options**—bright colors (yellow, red, or blue) are easier to spot in a cluttered cubby or classroom.
- **Built-in hook or loop** for hanging on a cubby hook (saves floor space). Not all foldable stools offer this, but some do.

### **Where to Use the Stool Beyond the Locker Cubby**
A foldable stool is a versatile tool for a single mom:
- **At home**: Reaching high kitchen cabinets, changing lightbulbs, or helping your child brush teeth at a counter.
- **On field trips**: Sitting for lunch, reaching bus seats, or as a temporary table.
- **After-school activities**: Dance recitals, soccer games, or library visits.
