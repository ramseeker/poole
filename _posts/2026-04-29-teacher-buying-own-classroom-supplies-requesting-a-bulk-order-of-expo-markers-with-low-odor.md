## Essential Classroom Supplies Shopping List

- **Expo Low-Odor Dry-Erase Markers (Bulk Set of 48 or 72)** – Fine or chisel tip
- Reusable Microfiber Eraser Cloths (pack of 12–24)
- Whiteboard Cleaning Spray (low-odor, or DIY vinegar-water solution)
- Magnetic Marker Holder or Storage Caddy (wall-mounted or desktop)
- Extra Whiteboard Eraser (felt-backed, large size)
- Replacement Whiteboard Cleaner Sponges (if applicable)

## Detailed Buying Guide

### 1. Expo Low-Odor Dry-Erase Markers (Bulk Set of 48 or 72)
**Why low-odor matters:** Traditional dry-erase markers contain strong solvents that can trigger headaches, asthma, or dizziness—especially in a classroom with 25+ students and poor ventilation. Low-odor markers (like Expo’s) use a water-based or ethanol-based ink with reduced volatile organic compounds (VOCs).  
**Logic for bulk:** A single marker lasts roughly 2–3 weeks under daily use (whiteboard lessons, group work, student practice). A bulk set of 48 ensures you have enough for the entire school year without running out mid-lesson. Buying in bulk also cuts per-marker cost by 30–50% compared to retail packs.  
**Tip:** Choose fine tip for detailed writing (math equations, diagrams) or chisel tip for bold headings and large-group visibility. Many teachers split the order: 30 fine + 18 chisel for versatility.

### 2. Reusable Microfiber Eraser Cloths (Pack of 12–24)
**Why cloths over erasers:** Standard felt erasers trap ink residue and require frequent cleaning, which can spread smudges. Microfiber cloths absorb ink without scratching the board, and they’re machine-washable (up to 200 washes before losing efficacy).  
**Logic for quantity:** You need at least one cloth per student group (5–6 groups = 6 cloths) plus backup cloths for your personal use at the front board. A pack of 12 covers a full year, assuming you launder them weekly. For 72-marker bulk orders, half the cloths can be designated “color-specific” (one for dark ink, one for light) to avoid color transfer.

### 3. Whiteboard Cleaning Spray (Low-Odor or DIY Mix)
**Why not just water:** Water alone leaves a film that makes future marks blurry. A proper low-odor cleaning spray (e.g., Expo Whiteboard Cleaner or a homemade 1:1 vinegar-water mix) dissolves dried ink and restores the board’s non-porous surface.  
**Logic for bulk supply:** A 32-ounce bottle typically lasts 2–3 months in a busy classroom. If you’re ordering markers in bulk, buy 2–3 bottles at once to avoid mid-year lapses. **Pro tip:** Label a spray bottle “Low-Odor Cleaner – Use Only on Whiteboard” to prevent accidental use on paper or plastic surfaces.

### 4. Magnetic Marker Holder or Storage Caddy
**Why storage matters:** Markers left on your desk or in open bins dry out faster due to exposure to air and heat. A magnetic holder attaches directly to the whiteboard rail, keeping markers horizontal (ink stays at the tip) and within arm’s reach.  
**Logic for structure:** A caddy with individual slots (e.g., 12–24 slots) prevents students from mixing caps or leaving markers uncapped. For a bulk order of 48 markers, you’ll need two caddies (one front board, one student station). **Avoid** vertical storage cups, as markers tend to clog when stored tip-down.

### 5. Extra Whiteboard Eraser (Felt-Backed, Large Size)
**Why a second eraser:** You’ll need one for daily group work and one for final board cleaning. Standard erasers (e.g., Expo Petit Eraser) are small and wear out quickly. A large felt-backed eraser (8” x 4”) lasts 6–8 months with daily use.  
**Logic for bulk order:** Add one eraser for every 12 markers you buy. This ensures you have one at the board, one for student stations, and a spare for when the felt gets matted (replace every 4–6 months).

### 6. Replacement Whiteboard Cleaner Sponges (if applicable)
**Why sponges:** Some teachers prefer a damp sponge for quick spot-cleaning between lessons. Standard kitchen sponges leave lint; dedicated whiteboard sponges (with abrasive side) remove stubborn ghosting.  
**Logic for bulk:** Order a 6-pack to replace sponges monthly (they harbor bacteria after 30 days of repeated damp use). This keeps your board sanitary for students with allergies or immune concerns.

Many retailers offer 10–15% off for teacher accounts or bulk educational purchases with a tax-exempt form. Store all markers horizontally in a cool, dark drawer (not near windows or radiators) to extend their lifespan to 12–18 months.
