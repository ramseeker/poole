## Buying Guide: Logic for Each Item

### Why UPF 50+ Is Non-Negotiable
For an older woman with sun sensitivity, **UPF 50+ means only 1/50th of UV radiation penetrates the fabric**—critical for preventing sunburn, photosensitivity reactions, and skin cancer risk. Unlike SPF (which measures UVB only), UPF rates both UVA and UVB. Any sleeve labeled below 50+ still allows harmful rays through; this list prioritizes certified UVA/UVB protection.

### Cooling Fabric Technology Matters
Older skin is thinner and less efficient at regulating temperature. **"Cooling" fabrics work via evaporative cooling (like bamboo viscose or ice silk) or moisture-wicking (like Dri-FIT)**. They pull sweat away from skin, then disperse it across the fabric surface to cool via evaporation. Avoid cotton-only sleeves—they absorb sweat but don’t dry fast, leading to heat rashes and discomfort.

### Sleeve Design and Fit
- **Adjustable thumbhole** (Coolibar Sombra): Prevents sleeves from riding up during movement—critical for gardening, driving, or short walks. The thumb loop also covers the back of the hand, often missed by sunscreen.
- **Elasticized top with silicone grip** (UV Skinz): Holds sleeves in place without tight bands that can leave red marks on delicate skin. Silicone strips reduce migration.
- **Compression fit** (Sunzel): Beneficial for swelling (edema) or poor circulation, common in older adults. Graduated compression (tighter at wrist, looser at upper arm) supports lymphatic drainage.

### Material Choices for Sensitive Skin
- **Cotton-blend** (Coolibar Sombra): Gentle for those with allergies to spandex or nylon. However, heavier cotton dries slowly—acceptable only for low-activity use.
- **Bamboo charcoal** (Solbari): Naturally antibacterial, hypoallergenic, and retains cooling even after multiple washes. Charcoal infusions don’t bleed or irritate.
- **Aloe vera infused** (UV Skinz): Provides extra moisture barrier for dry, sun-damaged skin. The aloe leaches out slightly during activity, offering mild skin relief.
- **Ice silk/polyester mesh** (Rayward): Ultra-lightweight and fast-drying—best for humid climates. Avoid if skin is very thin/tears easily, as rougher mesh may cause friction on papery skin.

### Size and Measurement Tips
Older women often have changes in arm circumference due to fluid retention or muscle loss. **Measure both the bicep (widest point) and wrist circumference before buying.** Sleeves with elastic bands (e.g., Solbari) compress slightly; those with flat snug fits (Sunzel) may be less forgiving. Look for **size charts that list "bicep up to X inches"** rather than one-size-mistakes.

### Care Instructions to Extend Life
UPF ratings degrade with washing if not cared for. **Cold wash only** (hot water breaks elastic fibers). **No fabric softener**—coats fibers, reducing sweat-wicking and cooling efficacy. **Line dry** or tumble dry on low; high heat shrinks synthetic blends and damages silicone grips.

### When to Choose Cooling vs. Thermal Sleeves
**Cooling sleeves** (like Rayward Ice Silk) work by evaporation—ideal in temperatures above 75°F with moderate humidity. For dry heat below 80°F, **bamboo (Solbari)** balances cooling with sun protection without evaporative chill that can feel uncomfortable on aging skin. Avoid "cooling" labels that are just marketing—check for **Qmax value** (a textile cooling measurement; 0.25+ is meaningful cooling).
